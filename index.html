The first fight never became a real fight: Van caught a kick and Pantoja dislocated his elbow while bracing the fall, ending the bout in 26 seconds. This rematch finally answers the matchup question.

The hinge: Van’s pace and power can drown opponents late, but Taira banked early wrestling rounds before Van rallied. Pantoja’s scrambling is at least as dangerous. If the champion keeps it in open space, his volume is difficult to oppose; if Pantoja creates repeat mat exchanges, the plus-money side has a real structural path.

Guide lean: Pantoja at +110, narrowly. The injury ending proved little, and his grappling directly attacks Van’s clearest visible vulnerability. Van remains the deserved favorite on youth, pace and improved takedown defense.
