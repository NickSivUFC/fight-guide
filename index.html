Silva was booked for Yair Rodriguez until an undisclosed injury forced Rodriguez out in August. Delgado accepted his first UFC main event—and first scheduled five-round fight—on roughly three to four weeks’ notice in his home state.

The hinge: Silva’s counter hooks and front-choke game meet Delgado’s stance switches, long-range counters and dangerous clinch. Delgado has conceded seven takedowns across his last two fights and sometimes shoots without a setup; Silva has the guillotine and ninja choke to punish that.

Analyst call: MMA Junkie’s Dan Tom picks Silva inside the distance, likely a club-and-sub by the end of Round 2, while explicitly calling Delgado a live underdog. The odds market and SI’s card pick also favor Silva.
